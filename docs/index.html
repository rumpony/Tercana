Features

Live subtitles

Pick the subtitle area once. New lines are translated in under a second, right over the original.

Clean output

Skips button prompts and HUD text. Keeps speaker names and highlights them.

Any text on screen

Letters, menus, item descriptions — one shortcut.

Private

Recognition and translation run on your Mac. Nothing goes to the cloud.

Trophies

Rarity, statistics, closest to platinum, pinned goals with notes, guide links.

Controller

DualSense battery in the menu bar, low-battery warnings over your game.

Languages

Getting started

  1. Download SubLens and move it to Applications.
  2. Open PS Remote Play and start your game.
  3. Press ⌃⌥L and drag over the subtitle area. Done — it remembers.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. SubLens is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does it need the internet?

No for translation: everything runs on your Mac once the language pack is downloaded. Trophies need a connection.

Which games work?

Any game with on-screen subtitles in PS Remote Play — and any video with subtitles.

Which languages?

Every language Apple's on-device recognition and translation support — more than 20.

Is it affiliated with Sony?

No. SubLens is an independent project.
