Get started in a minute

  1. Download SubLens and move it to Applications.
  2. Open PS Remote Play and start your game.
  3. Press ⌃⌥L and drag over the subtitle area. Done — it remembers.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. SubLens is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does it need the internet?

No for translation: everything runs on your Mac once the language pack is downloaded. Trophies need a connection.

Which games work?

Any game with on-screen subtitles in PS Remote Play — and any video with subtitles.

Which languages?

Every language Apple's on-device recognition and translation support — more than 20.

Is it affiliated with Sony?

No. SubLens is an independent project.
